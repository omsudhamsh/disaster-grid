# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Disaster Grid is intended to be a respectful, inclusive, and constructive project for students, developers, researchers, emergency-technology enthusiasts, and other contributors.

We are committed to providing a welcoming environment where people can contribute regardless of background, experience level, or role.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Communicate constructively and assume good intent.
- Give useful, actionable feedback.
- Respect different technical approaches and experience levels.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Protect sensitive information, credentials, personal data, and potentially sensitive disaster-response information.
- Follow GitHub's Terms of Service and applicable laws.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, API keys, tokens, or other secrets.
- Malicious code, intentionally harmful changes, or attempts to compromise systems.
- Spam or repeated off-topic promotion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, close, or reject contributions or discussions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions on participation.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at **omsudhamshp@zohomail.in**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, please open a GitHub issue only when the report does not contain sensitive information.

For security vulnerabilities, do **not** open a public issue. Follow the instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces where contributors are representing the Disaster Grid project.
